Frozen intent contract C0 (source-grounded, pre-candidate)
- For distinct occupied keys return mutable references to those entries in input order; writing one must not change others.
- Allocated capacity is not initialized entry length: never form an Entry reference at an uninitialized index. Return IndexOutOfBounds there, IndexVacant at a vacancy, and OverlappingIndices for duplicate keys.
- Removal and reinsertion must preserve all unaffected values and ordinary exactly-once destruction.
